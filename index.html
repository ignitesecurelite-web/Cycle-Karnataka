What needs internet?

Needs internet: map tiles (OpenStreetMap), place search (Nominatim), cycling routes (FOSSGIS bike routing server).
Works without internet: GPS tracking, ride history, GPX/CSV export (once the page itself is loaded).
Not implemented: offline maps, offline routing, elevation, live turn-by-turn voice guidance.
